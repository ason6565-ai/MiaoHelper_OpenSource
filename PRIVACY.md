# Privacy Policy — MiaoHelper

Last updated: 2026-09-16

MiaoHelper is a free, ad-free Android accessibility tool that helps you rewrite and translate text in your preferred style. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data is processed and how.

## 1. Local-first by design

Most processing happens on your device. Your input text, local dictionaries, glossary terms, custom personas, translation cache and app logs are stored locally on your device only. Nothing in this category leaves your device, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## 2. Accessibility Service

MiaoHelper uses the Android Accessibility Service to read text from the screen you are currently interacting with, for the sole purpose of rewriting or translating that text. We do not record, store, or transmit this text beyond what you explicitly trigger (see section 3).

## 3. AI Rewrite (optional)

When you use the "Full Rewrite" feature, the selected text is sent to a third-party AI service to generate a rewritten version. This happens only when you explicitly trigger it, and only for the text you select. You can stop using this feature at any time. The AI service may process your text according to its own policies; we encourage you to review them.

## 4. No data collection

MiaoHelper has no accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK, and no cross-app tracking. We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information.

## 5. Permissions

- Accessibility Service: used to read on-screen text for rewriting/translation.
- Overlay / floating window: used to show the floating action button.

No other sensitive permissions are requested.

## 6. Children's privacy

MiaoHelper is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The latest version will always be available at this page.
